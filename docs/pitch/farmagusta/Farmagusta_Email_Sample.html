Invitation to bowel cancer screening

You are invited to take part in bowel cancer screening. The test looks for hidden blood in a stool sample. You take the sample at home. Participation is voluntary.

3. Book a pickup and prepare the package.
Choose a robot pickup window. Seal your sample package and follow the collection status in the app.

4. Open your result when notified.
The app shows your result and any next steps recorded by the screening team.
