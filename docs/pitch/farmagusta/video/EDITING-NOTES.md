## Editorial choices

The story is invitation → home test → booking → tracking → hospital oversight → physical handoff → robot departure. The hospital section should show collection coordination and waiting samples, not claim that this newly collected sample already has a lab result.

The first robot shot is a short visual hook; the longer departure belongs at the end. Source clips 0205, 0207, 0208, 0209 and 0210 supply the real action. Source audio is muted in the cuts because it is location/crew sound; all original sound remains available for a later sound edit. No music or voice-over has been added.

HDR footage is converted to SDR for a consistent, shareable H.264 MP4. The exported edit does not copy phone GPS metadata. Keep the original files for a later colour/audio pass.

The closing QR was verified from the invitation PDF and points to https://farmagusta.vercel.app/kit. The video uses the updated invitation, not the old placeholder-address version.
