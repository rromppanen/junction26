## What they said

1. **Not one recommendation, a tool for weighing options** [0:00–0:19]. Instead of "you should do exactly this", build a tool where the user can compare, e.g. *if we raise screening by a certain amount, how much does it cost*, balance the trade-offs and find an optimal solution.
2. **Cost-benefit decides** [0:19–0:49]. Some screenings are not adopted because they are considered too expensive: X people screened finds only so many cancers, even though it matters a lot for that population. Keep cost in mind in whatever solution you build.
3. **The user group is ours to decide** [1:04]. County leaders, hospitals, public or private actors; "probably public, but you decide", and look at the problem from that point of view.
4. **Patients can't demand what they can't see** [1:31–1:58]. A patient can't see where they should be on the pathway, what happens next or how long a delay is allowed. Staff change along the pathway and there is no personal-doctor model yet, so the patient can't demand anything either.
5. **Treatment guidelines exist, but nobody monitors them** [2:13–2:43]. Care is reviewed afterwards, when the data is already old.
6. **Make the strategy real, not theoretical; don't try to solve everything at once** [2:43–2:58].

## What this means for farmagusta

farmagusta, the product we submit, takes these points as follows:

- **Point 4 (the patient can't see the next step)** is now the core: the phone app shows the person each step, from the invitation to the handover, in their own language.
- **Point 5 (nobody monitors)**: the nurse board flags everyone who falls behind, including a positive test with no colonoscopy booked after 7 days.
- **Point 3 (user group)**: we chose public screening services, with the invited person and the screening nurse as users.
- **Points 1–2 (cost and options)**: covered only by the postage comparison and the pilot, which prices a robot pickup against a return envelope. farmagusta does not compare options for a county.
- **Point 6 (don't solve everything)**: one programme, one city, one pilot.
